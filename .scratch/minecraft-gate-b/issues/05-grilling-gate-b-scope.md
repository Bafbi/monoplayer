# Grilling: Gate B acceptance and scope

**Type:** grilling
**Status:** open
**Blocked by:** 01, 02, 03, 04

## Question

After the research lands, settle the values that shape Gate B:

- What is the minimum proof that Gate B passes? Which failures are fatal, and
  which only descope a lane?
- Is the mandatory backend set vanilla only, vanilla + Paper, or all three
  (vanilla, Paper, Fabric)? What does each additional backend buy?
- Is the server-side proxy mandatory, optional, or a later compatibility lane?
- What level of client automation is required: manual official-client run,
  scripted GUI, or a Fabric test mod? What is allowed to be “manual” in the
  gate?
- Who owns the two accounts, where do tokens live, and what is the fallback if
  only one account is available?
- Is the decompiled-source workflow a Gate B requirement or an engineering
  convenience?
- Does Gate B run locally first, in `monoplayer-dev`, or both?

Context: the four research notes are in `../research/`, the three council
memos are in `../council/`, and the draft plan is
[`docs/gate-b-plan.html`](../../../docs/gate-b-plan.html).
